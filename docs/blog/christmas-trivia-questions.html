Christmas Trivia Questions: 50 Fun Questions for Families (With Answers)

Santa, reindeer, holiday movies, festive food and traditions from around the world, all in one Christmas quiz

Christmas is the one time of year when the whole family ends up around the same table: grandparents, cousins, little ones and teenagers who would usually be on their phones. A round of Christmas trivia is one of the easiest ways to get everyone talking, laughing and competing (politely) for bragging rights until next December.

We've put together 50 Christmas trivia questions with answers, split into five festive categories. There are easy questions for young kids, trickier ones for grown-ups, and a few surprises that will stump even the biggest Christmas fan in your house. Tap "Show Answer" to reveal each one.

How to Host a Christmas Trivia Game

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Mix Up the Teams

Pair a younger child with a grandparent or an older cousin. Kids usually know the movies, while adults know the history, so mixed teams stay close all game long.

🎁 Play for Festive Prizes

Chocolate coins, the first pick from the cookie plate or the right to choose the next Christmas movie all make great prizes.

🕯️ Pick the Right Moment

Christmas Eve after dinner, while the turkey is roasting, or on a long drive to see relatives are all perfect times for a quick round.

⭐ Let Everyone Pick a Category

Give each player or team a turn choosing the next category below. Movie fans and foodies both get their moment to shine.

Tips for a Merry Trivia Night

1. Give Little Ones a Head Start

Read questions aloud for younger kids and offer two or three answer choices when a question is tricky. A quick win keeps them excited for the next round.

2. Save the Hardest Questions for a Tie-Breaker

Questions like the total number of gifts in "The Twelve Days of Christmas" make perfect tie-breakers. Closest guess wins.

3. Share the Story Behind the Answer

Did you know that both male and female reindeer grow antlers? Little facts like this spark conversations and turn a quiz into a family memory.

4. Make It a Yearly Tradition

Crown a Christmas trivia champion every year. A paper crown from a Christmas cracker makes the perfect trophy.
