Spiders

  1. Easy How many legs does an adult spider have?
    Show answer

    Eight. Count four pairs.

  2. Challenge Which two body features help you tell a spider from an adult insect?
    Show answer

    A spider has eight legs and two main body sections. An adult insect has six legs and three main sections.

The night sky

  1. Easy Which planet is famous for its bright rings?
    Show answer

    Saturn. Its rings are wide enough to see with a small telescope.

  2. Challenge What are most of the particles in Saturn’s rings made of?
    Show answer

    Water ice. Sunlight reflecting from the ice makes the rings look bright.

The kitchen

  1. Easy Which part of a plant is a carrot: a root, leaf or flower?
    Show answer

    A root. The part we usually eat grows underground.

  2. Challenge Which spice comes from the bark of a tree?
    Show answer

    Cinnamon. Thin strips of bark curl as they dry.

Music

  1. Easy How many strings does a standard violin have?
    Show answer

    Four. Players usually draw a bow across them.

  2. Challenge Which is the lowest-pitched instrument in a standard string quartet?
    Show answer

    The cello. A string quartet has two violins, one viola and one cello.

Numbers

  1. Easy How many sides does a hexagon have?
    Show answer

    Six. A honeycomb cell is a familiar example.

  2. Challenge What is the smallest prime number greater than ten?
    Show answer

    Eleven. Its only positive divisors are one and eleven.

Oceans

  1. Easy Which is the largest ocean on Earth?
    Show answer

    The Pacific. It lies between the Americas and Asia and Australia.

  2. Challenge What kind of water forms where a freshwater river mixes with seawater?
    Show answer

    Brackish water. It is saltier than fresh water and less salty than seawater.

Books

  1. Easy What does a table of contents help you find?
    Show answer

    Chapters or sections and where they begin. It is usually at the front.

  2. Challenge Which part of a reference book lists topics alphabetically with their page numbers?
    Show answer

    The index. Look for it near the back.

On the field

  1. Easy How many points is a free throw worth in basketball?
    Show answer

    One. The player shoots from the free-throw line.

  2. Challenge A basketball team scores twice from beyond the three-point line and makes one free throw. How many points is that?
    Show answer

    Seven. Two threes plus one makes seven.

Weather

  1. Easy What do we call water that freezes and falls from clouds in flakes?
    Show answer

    Snow. A snowflake is made of ice crystals.

  2. Challenge Which instrument measures atmospheric pressure?
    Show answer

    A barometer. Changes in pressure can help forecasters follow weather systems.

Time

  1. Easy How many days are there in a leap year?
    Show answer

    366. February gets the extra day.

  2. Challenge Which year was a leap year: 1900 or 2000?
    Show answer

    2000. A century year in the Gregorian calendar is a leap year when it is divisible by 400.
