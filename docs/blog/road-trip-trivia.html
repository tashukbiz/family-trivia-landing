30 Road Trip Trivia Questions for Kids and Adults

Let a passenger host while the driver keeps their eyes on the road. Start with the easy question in each pair, then let someone else try the challenge. Give a point for each answer.

Reading the road

  1. Easy How many sides does a standard red stop sign have?
    Show answer

    Eight. Count them around the edge.

  2. Challenge What is the mathematical name for that eight-sided shape?
    Show answer

    An octagon. Its name starts with the same prefix as octopus.

  3. Easy Which direction is opposite east on a compass?
    Show answer

    West. East and west sit across from each other.

  4. Challenge If the top of a map is north, which direction is the left edge?
    Show answer

    West. Turn the map to face the same way as your compass to check.

  5. Easy What do you call a road that goes underneath a mountain?
    Show answer

    A tunnel. The mountain stays above you.

  6. Challenge What do you call the part of a bridge that crosses the gap between two supports?
    Show answer

    A span. A long bridge can have several spans.

  7. Easy How many minutes are in half an hour?
    Show answer

    Thirty. Half of 60 is 30.

  8. Challenge A trip takes 90 minutes. If you leave at 2:15, when do you arrive in the same time zone?
    Show answer

    3:45. Add one hour, then another 30 minutes.

  9. Easy What is the book of maps in a glove compartment called?
    Show answer

    An atlas. It can hold road maps for a whole country.

  10. Challenge On a map, 1 centimetre stands for 5 kilometres. How far apart are towns drawn 3 centimetres apart?
    Show answer

    15 kilometres. Multiply the map distance by five.

Through the passenger window

  1. Easy What is the name of a river that flows into a larger river?
    Show answer

    A tributary. Follow it downstream and it joins the larger river.

  2. Challenge What do we call the point where two rivers meet?
    Show answer

    A confluence. Look for a Y shape on a map.

  3. Easy Which bright object lights the Moon?
    Show answer

    The Sun. Moonlight is reflected sunlight.

  4. Challenge Why does the Moon sometimes look like a crescent?
    Show answer

    From Earth we see only part of its sunlit half. The amount we see changes as the Moon orbits Earth.

  5. Easy What is a group of sheep in a field called?
    Show answer

    A flock. Farmers also use the word for a group of birds.

  6. Challenge What is the word for a mammal that eats plants?
    Show answer

    A herbivore. Sheep and cows are examples.

  7. Easy What has to hit raindrops for you to see a daytime rainbow?
    Show answer

    Sunlight. A shower with the Sun behind you is a good time to look.

  8. Challenge Why do two passengers see a flash of lightning before they hear its thunder?
    Show answer

    Light travels faster than sound. Both come from the same lightning event.

  9. Easy What do we call land surrounded by water on all sides?
    Show answer

    An island. It can be in a lake, river or ocean.

  10. Challenge What do we call land surrounded by water on three sides?
    Show answer

    A peninsula. One side connects it to the mainland.

Places worth a detour

  1. Easy Which city is home to the Eiffel Tower?
    Show answer

    Paris, in France.

  2. Challenge Which river runs through Paris past the Eiffel Tower?
    Show answer

    The Seine. It flows through the city towards the English Channel.

  3. Easy Which country has a mainland shaped like a boot?
    Show answer

    Italy. Its toe points towards Sicily.

  4. Challenge Which is the largest island in the Mediterranean Sea?
    Show answer

    Sicily. It sits just off the toe of Italy.

  5. Easy In which US state would you visit the Grand Canyon?
    Show answer

    Arizona. The national park is in the northern part of the state.

  6. Challenge Which river flows through the Grand Canyon?
    Show answer

    The Colorado River. You can see it from some viewpoints on the rim.

  7. Easy Which Australian city has an opera house shaped like white sails?
    Show answer

    Sydney. The building stands beside Sydney Harbour.

  8. Challenge Which city is the capital of Australia?
    Show answer

    Canberra. Sydney is the capital of the state of New South Wales.

  9. Easy On which continent would you visit the pyramids of Giza?
    Show answer

    Africa. The pyramids stand in Egypt.

  10. Challenge Which sea would you reach by following the Nile River downstream to its delta?
    Show answer

    The Mediterranean Sea. The river reaches it on the northern coast of Egypt.
